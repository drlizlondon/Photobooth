What the photobooth is

MyBishBash Photobooth is a web app that uses your device's camera to take three photos and turn them into a photo strip, a magazine cover and a Living Polaroid. It runs in your browser. There is no app to install and no account to create.

Using it

You may use the photobooth for your own events. Please do not use it to photograph people who have not agreed to be photographed, and please respect the law and the venue you are in. You are responsible for the photos you take and for what you do with them afterwards.

Your photos are yours

You keep all rights in the photographs you take. We do not receive them, do not store them and make no claim to them. See Privacy for exactly where they live.

Your data lives on your device — and only there

This is the most important practical thing to understand. The booth stores your photos and event settings in your browser on the device you are using. That means:

  • There is no backup. We cannot recover anything for you.
  • Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or your browser reclaiming space can remove them permanently.
  • Your booth does not follow you to another device or another browser.

If the photos matter, save or share them off the device while you still can.
