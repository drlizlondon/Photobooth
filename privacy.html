Privacy

Last updated 11 August 2026. This describes what the photobooth does today, not what it may do later.

The photobooth runs entirely in your browser. Your photos are taken, edited and saved on your own device. They are not uploaded to us, and we do not have a copy of them.

What happens when you use the booth

  • The camera. The booth asks your browser for camera access and captures video only — audio is never requested or recorded.
  • Your photos. The three photos, the photo strip, the magazine cover and the Living Polaroid are all created in your browser. They are stored on your device in your browser's local storage so that the booth can show them again between guests.
  • Your event settings. The event name, date, colour and wording you enter are stored on your device in the same way.
  • Nothing is sent to us. Using the booth makes no request to any MyBishBash server, and no request to anyone else's.

Saving and sharing — please read this bit

Save writes the image or video to your device, as any download would.

Share hands the file to your device's own share sheet. What happens after that is entirely your choice, and outside our control — if you send a photo to a messaging app, a social network or an email, that service receives the file and its own privacy terms apply.

We say this plainly because "your photos never leave your device" would not be accurate: they stay on your device as far as the booth is concerned, and they leave only when you deliberately send them somewhere.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no password.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no profiling.
  • No cookies are set by the photobooth.
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or content are loaded. The booth makes no requests to any other company's servers.
  • No cloud storage of guest photographs.
  • No email addresses are collected. Nothing on the site asks for one.

Payments

Nothing is currently on sale, so no payment is processed and no payment details are handled at all. When paid plans open, payment will be taken by a payment provider who receives the payment details directly — we would not see or store card details — and this page will be updated to say exactly who that is before anything goes on sale.

Business events

The Business version of the product is designed to record an attendee's consent decisions — and, only where an organiser has enabled it and the attendee has separately given permission, to collect photographs. None of that is running. The server component it needs is not deployed, no Business event has been configured, and no attendee data has ever been collected. This section will be rewritten with specifics before that changes.

Removing your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you are in control of it. Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes the photos, the gallery and your event settings. There is no copy anywhere else for us to delete, and no backup — so if you clear it, or use private browsing, it is gone.

Your rights and contact

Since we do not hold your photos or any account, there is generally nothing for us to look up, correct or delete on request. If you have a question about privacy, or believe something here is inaccurate, contact us and we will put it right.
